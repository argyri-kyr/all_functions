This line below exists in loaduncutdata_hardcases. This was our first set of rules that takes only gazes that have x,y positive
#( leftvalid[-1]==1 and rightvalid[-1]==1 and leftx[-1]>0 and lefty[-1]>0 and rightx[-1]>0 and righty[-1]>0 ):
But, we came across some cases where one of the two eyes had negative values but nevertheless the recording was ok to use. 
Thus we changed it to this, which takes account only the validity from the eye tracker and not the x,y coordinates of the eyes
but won't be bothred by the "-1" values bucause the "-1" values goes hand-in-hand with 0 validity. 
#( leftvalid[-1]==1 and rightvalid[-1]==1 ):

Η βασικη συναρτηση για την διαχειριση των raw gazes ειναι η loaduncutdata_hardcases και η loaduncutdata αντιστοιχα.
H gazeshandling mas bgazei ta .txt arxeia tou vergence kai tou gazespy, tha psaksoume na allaksoume to gazespy na 
bgainei apo tis sinartiseis loaduncutdata_hardcases kai loaduncutdata antistoixa.
