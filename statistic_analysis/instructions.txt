To run a single student:
1. Vale to fakelo tou subject mesa sto Folder: R:\individual (Profile_cards_db (\\MREYE2))
2. Ean exeis diathesimi DOB ftiaxe ena .txt ths morfis  YYYY-MM-DD.txt mesa se ena opoiodhpote Gazesfolder h vale to sto GUI*
3. Trekse to run_analysis_main.py

* Ean grapseis DOB, kai valeis range of studentes, den tha doulepsei.



Note: min exeis allous fakelous mesa sto fakelo tou individual giati to 'one-page-sheet-indiv_v7.0.py' tha krasarei
Prepei na to ftiaksoume. Ean o fakelos einai ths morfs kati_kati_allo tote einai OK

To run many students:
1. Vale to fakelo twn subject mesa sto Folder: R:\individual (Profile_cards_db (\\MREYE2))
2. Trekse to script 'create_birth_txt_from_db.py', gia na valeis DOB texts se polous mazi. Xreiazetai na ftaikseis ena excel file me ID kai DOBs.
3. Trekse to run_analysis_main.py
